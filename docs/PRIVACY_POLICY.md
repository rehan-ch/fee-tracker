# Fee Tracker – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

## Summary

Fee Tracker stores your records on your phone. We do not sell or share your data, and the
app has no advertising, analytics or tracking. Backups, reminders and receipts are only
created when you tap a button. If you choose to turn on cloud sync (where available), the
business you choose is stored in our cloud database so your staff can use it too.

## Information you enter

Your business name and phone number; for each customer: name, phone number, address,
area, notes, monthly fee, fee changes and payment records (amount, date, method, who
received it and a note); staff names, phone numbers and roles; your reminder message and
payment details. This is saved in the app's private storage on your phone and is used only
to show your collection lists, balances, reminders, receipts and reports.

## Staff PINs

Staff PINs are stored only on the phone where they were set, as a salted one-way hash. They
are never included in backup files or cloud sync.

## Reminders and receipts

When you send a reminder or a receipt, the app opens WhatsApp, your SMS app or the Android
share menu with the text or file filled in, and you send it yourself. The app cannot read
your messages. Those apps handle what you send under their own privacy policies.

## Alerts

If you turn on alerts, the app schedules notifications on your phone (for example "15
customers still unpaid"). They are created on the phone; nothing is sent to a server.
Android asks for your permission first.

## Backups

A backup file is created only when you tap **Back up now**. Android then shows the share
menu, and you choose where to send or save the file (for example Google Drive). That
service handles the file under its own privacy policy. The backup file is not encrypted, so
keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a backup file that you pick.

## Android device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app's data in
your device backup to your Google account. This is managed by Google and your device
settings; we cannot access it.

## Cloud sync (optional)

Cloud sync is not available in every version of the app, and it is off unless you turn it
on. When you sign in, we store your email address to
send you sign-in codes. When you turn sync on for a business, that business's records
listed above (except PINs) are stored in our cloud database, hosted by Supabase, so the
owner's and staff phones stay up to date. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS)
and only accounts you invite as staff can see it, with the role you give them. We use this
data only to provide sync, never for advertising, and we do not sell it.

You can remove a business from the cloud, or delete your cloud account and the businesses
you own in it, from **Settings → Cloud sync**, or ask for deletion on our
[account deletion page](https://rehan-ch.github.io/fee-tracker/delete-account.html). Deleted data is removed from our database straight away; our
hosting provider's backups may keep a copy for a short time until they are replaced.

## Permissions

Internet (only used for cloud sync, if you turn it on) and notifications (only if you turn
on alerts). The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone,
photos, files or SMS. The **Call** button opens your phone's dialer with the number filled
in; you place the call yourself.

## Your customers' information

You are responsible for the customer details you record. If a customer asks you to remove
their details, open the customer and tap **Delete customer**.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a single customer, a whole business (**Settings → Businesses**), or
everything on the phone at once (**Settings → Delete all data**). Uninstalling the app also
removes all data stored by the app on your phone. Backup files and messages you sent
elsewhere must be deleted where you saved or sent them. Cloud data is deleted as described
above.

## Children

This app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app and on this page,
