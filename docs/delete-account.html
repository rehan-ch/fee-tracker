Fee Tracker – Delete your account and data

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Fee Tracker is published by Rehan Ch. This page explains how to delete the data the app holds about you, and how to delete your cloud account if you use cloud sync.

Data on your phone

Everything you enter in Fee Tracker (customers, fees, payments, staff, settings) is stored on your phone. You can delete it yourself at any time:

  • One customer: open the customer and tap Delete customer.
  • One business: Settings → Businesses, then delete the business.
  • Everything: Settings → Delete all data. This removes every business, customer, payment and setting from the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all data stored by the app on your phone.

Backup files you saved (for example to Google Drive) and messages or receipts you sent through WhatsApp or SMS are outside the app. Delete them where you saved or sent them.

Cloud account (cloud sync)

If your version of the app has cloud sync and you signed in, you can delete your cloud account from inside the app:

  1. Open Settings → Cloud sync.
  2. Tap Delete cloud account and confirm.

This deletes your account, your email address and every business you own in the cloud, straight away. Data on your phone is not affected. Businesses you were invited to as staff stay with their owner; only your membership is removed.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email nexondev.ai@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Fee Tracker account deletion". We delete the account and its data within 7 days and confirm by email. We do not keep any of it afterwards; our hosting provider's backups may hold a copy for a short time until they are replaced.
